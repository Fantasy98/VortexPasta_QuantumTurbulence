Executed three-case results: 15 October 2026
============================================

All three cases use PredefinedCurves, periodic Biot-Savart mesh 64^3,
R=1, L=2*pi, Gamma=2.0826013772617338, a=0.001, Delta=0.615.
No imposed flow or mutual friction. Every case reached physical t=10 exactly.

Summed simulation-driver time: 89.26 minutes.
Peak measured RSS per process: 1.077 GiB.
Two Julia threads per process initially, at most two simulations concurrently.
The final trefoil continuation used four threads after the other cases finished.
BLAS/OpenMP were limited to one thread. No remote jobs were used.

The standalone entry scripts are single_ring.jl, hopf_link.jl, trefoil.jl.
analyze.py CASE generates case-dependent plots and a physical-time MP4.
See README.txt for launch, continuation, and analysis instructions.

  ./run.sh hopf_link --resume
  ./run.sh trefoil
  VORTEX_THREADS=4 ./run.sh trefoil --resume

The 20-minute per-invocation guard required one continuation for the Hopf link
and trefoil. No physical parameter or resolution was changed on continuation.
Full checkpoint state/counters were retained. Restart comparisons are in
validation/hopf_restart_continuity.json and trefoil_restart_continuity.json.
Per-segment source copies and configs preserve the actual loaded code.

The trefoil has significant numerical losses through reconnection, refinement,
and unresolved-loop removal. Its late helicity evolution is not a converged
physical/GPE decay result. These are three base trajectories, not a new
resolution or reconnection-sensitivity study. No GPE/paper curves are imported.
Its final energy is 19.87% below the initial value; H/Gamma^2 changes from
-3.35412 to -0.91094. These changes must be interpreted with the loss counters.

Native helicity: H=Gamma integral(v_BS.ds), reported as H/Gamma^2.
Geometric linking-plus-writhe is approximate and omits periodic-image Gauss terms.
The ring travels Delta z=13.6844813795, with speed 1.3684481380.

Driver times above omit package loading before driver initialization. The
external trefoil_resources.txt elapsed time additionally includes its initial
queue wait, so it should not be interpreted as active simulation time.
Original diagnostics, checkpoints, logs, and preliminary plotting checks are
preserved. The earlier 01, 02, and 03 experiment directories were not modified.
